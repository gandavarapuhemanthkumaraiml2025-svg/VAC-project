# ROOTWISE — Horticultural Sources & Research Bibliography
## Academic & Agricultural Citations

The RootWise advisory matrix is grounded in empirical research and peer-reviewed horticultural publications from leading universities and agricultural extension services.

---

### Primary Citations

1. **Royal Horticultural Society (RHS)**
   - *Title:* Houseplant Watering and Moisture Management Guide (2023)
   - *URL:* https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/for-places/houseplants
   - *Scope:* Establishes baseline moisture tolerances for tropical foliage (Monstera, Pothos, Peace Lily) and succulent groups (Snake Plant, ZZ Plant).
   - *Key Heuristic:* "Allow the top third of potting compost to dry between waterings for tropical foliage; allow complete dryout for arid succulents."

2. **University of Minnesota Extension (UMN)**
   - *Title:* Watering Indoor Plants: Principles of Soil Moisture and Aeration
   - *URL:* https://extension.umn.edu/houseplants/watering-houseplants
   - *Scope:* Investigates rhizosphere oxygenation, pot drainage physics, and root rot etiology (*Pythium*, *Phytophthora*).
   - *Key Heuristic:* "Containers lacking drainage holes trap perched water tables at the pot base, precipitating anoxic root mortality regardless of surface dryness."

3. **USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS)**
   - *Title:* Soil Quality Indicators: Evapotranspiration and Infiltration Dynamics
   - *URL:* https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/resources/guides-and-instructions/soil-health-guides
   - *Scope:* Soil substrate moisture retention comparisons across peat, coir, sand, and clay.
   - *Key Heuristic:* "Peat-based media undergo severe hydrophobic compaction upon desiccation below 15% moisture, necessitating bottom capillary subirrigation."

4. **Clemson Cooperative Extension**
   - *Title:* Tomato and Vegetable Irrigation Scheduling in Variable Climates
   - *URL:* https://hgic.clemson.edu/factsheet/watering-vegetable-gardens/
   - *Scope:* Weather-linked irrigation timing, blossom-end rot prevention, and fruit-set moisture consistency.
   - *Key Heuristic:* "Fluctuating moisture during fruit swelling induces radial cracking; postpone irrigation if anticipated rainfall exceeds 5mm within 24 hours."

5. **Texas A&M AgriLife Extension**
   - *Title:* Heat Stress and Evaporative Demand in Urban Horticulture
   - *URL:* https://aggie-horticulture.tamu.edu/
   - *Scope:* Meteorological thresholds, vapor pressure deficit (VPD), and ambient temperature influence on watering frequency.
   - *Key Heuristic:* "Ambient temperatures exceeding 32°C amplify transpiration by 200–300%, requiring proactive morning hydration before peak midday irradiance."
